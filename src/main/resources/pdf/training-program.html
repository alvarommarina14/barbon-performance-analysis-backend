RPE GUIDE — HOW TO CHOOSE THE RIGHT WEIGHT

Rate of Perceived Exertion — your built-in load self-regulator
RPE stands for "Rate of Perceived Exertion." It's a simple way to measure how hard a set feels WITHOUT needing to know your max weight. Instead of telling you exactly how much to lift, RPE tells you how hard the set should FEEL. The key concept is "Reps Left in the Tank" — how many more reps could you have done with good technique before you had to stop? If you do 10 reps and feel like you could have done 2 or 3 more with good form, you had 2-3 reps left in the tank. How to use it: After each set, ask yourself — "How many more reps could I have done with PERFECT form?" If the answer matches the target for that week, you picked the right weight. If it was too easy, go heavier next set. If you couldn't complete the reps, go lighter.
